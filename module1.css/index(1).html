Community Bulletin

The city council has approved a new parks development plan that will add three green spaces across the western districts. Construction is expected to begin early next year and will include walking trails, picnic areas, and children's playgrounds. Residents are encouraged to attend the next town hall for a detailed briefing.

The annual community awards ceremony will be held on the last Friday of this month. Nominations are now open for categories including Volunteer of the Year, Best Local Business, and Outstanding Youth Contribution. Submit your nominations via the online portal or drop a form at the community centre.

Road maintenance works on Elm Street will continue through the weekend. Motorists are advised to use Oak Avenue as an alternative route. The council apologises for any inconvenience and temporary traffic lights will be in place at key intersections during peak hours.
